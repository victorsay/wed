How to Write a Research Project Report

A practical guide for structuring clear, reproducible research writing.

A research project report should open with a clear title, abstract, and introduction that state the problem, purpose, and research questions.

Next, summarize relevant literature and explain your methods—design, data sources, tools, and analysis—so others can follow or reproduce your work.

Present findings objectively with tables or figures where helpful, then discuss what they mean, how they relate to prior research, and any limitations.

Close with conclusions and practical recommendations, and include a reference list plus appendices for raw data or extra material.

Keep the tone formal, structure sections with clear headings, and revise for clarity, accuracy, and consistent citation style.
